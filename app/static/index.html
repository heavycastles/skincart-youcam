From one selfie to a basket that fits your skin.

SkinCart scores 12 skin concerns with YouCam AI Skin Analysis, builds a routine from the shop's own catalog, previews what that routine targets on your photo, finds your foundation shade and lets you try it on before you order.

JPG or PNG, face filling most of the frame, good light. Photos are sent only to the YouCam API for analysis; results are cached per image, so a re-scan costs nothing.

        How it works

        1 · Upload

        YouCam File API stores the selfie once; every task reuses the same file id.

        2 · Analyze

        Skin Analysis scores 12 concerns and returns overlay masks.

        3 · Match & preview

        One product per step, an AM/PM plan, Skin Simulation of what the basket targets, and a foundation shade from Facial Color Tones.

        4 · Try & order

        Makeup VTO renders shades; the order becomes a scored lead.
